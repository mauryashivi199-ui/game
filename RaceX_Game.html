Game Features

👤
Login & Profile
Create your account, choose your avatar (female or male), set your racer name and customize your profile.
🚗
Car Customization
Unlock different cars — change color, body kit, rims. Earn new cars by winning races.
🌐
Online Multiplayer
Race real players worldwide in real-time. Up to 8 players per race via WebSocket live sync.
👭
Play with Friends
Create a private room, share code with friends. Race together and see who's fastest!
🌄
Stunning Tracks
5 environments — City Night, Desert, Mountain, Forest, Neon Tunnel — all animated.
🏆
Leaderboard
Global rankings, daily challenges, season trophies. Rookie to Legend!
